Hire directly. No middlemen.
Nothing stored. Ever.

Jobs are broadcast to contractors in the lobby and fulfilled over an encrypted, browser‑to‑browser WebRTC channel. Signaling touches a stateless edge worker for milliseconds — then the connection, the job, and the chat exist only in RAM on the two machines doing business.
