A job board that forgets.

Clients post a job from a template. Contractors bid with the payment they want. The client accepts one contractor — or rejects everyone — and accepted pairs move into a direct chat the client holds open. When a post is filled, rejected or abandoned, it and its bids are garbage collected. Nothing is stored.
